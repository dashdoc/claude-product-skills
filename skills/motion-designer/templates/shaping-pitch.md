# Template — shaping pitch

Overrides the matching steps of `SKILL.md`; everything not mentioned here follows it.

- **Slug**: the pitch name, kebab-case.
- **Language**: English (all Dashdoc communications are in English).

## Gate 1 — The brief

- **Resources**: the Notion pitch / roadmap card URL, the FigJam shaping board, and screenshots of prototypes or designs. The card's Problem, Success criteria and Scope are the core source.
- **Runtime**: between 1:00 and 1:30, no exceptions.
- **Audience**: ask which one — **betting table** (convince them to bet) or **roadmap review** (show what's coming). It sets the tone: the betting table hears the problem and the stakes, the roadmap review sees the solution in action.
- **Format**: landscape.
- **Claim**: user value first, in the customer's words, pulled from the pitch's Problem and Success criteria, e.g. "Dispatchers see every late truck before the customer calls."

## Gate 6 — Final

If the roadmap card is known, offer to upload the video to it (Notion file upload, then a video block at the top of the card body); upload only on a yes.
